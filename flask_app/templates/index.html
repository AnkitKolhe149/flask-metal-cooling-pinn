⚛️ Governing Physics

The PINN enforces Newton's effective cooling law as a hard constraint during training:

dθ/dτ + β · θ = 0

θ = (T − T_bath) / (T₀ − T_bath) [dimensionless temperature]
τ = t / t_max [dimensionless time]
β [learnable cooling coefficient]

The analytical solution is θ(τ) = e^(−βτ). The PINN learns β from data while simultaneously fitting the cooling curve, enforcing physical consistency throughout the domain.

📰 Research Highlights

✓ Cooling Behaviour

Cooling curves were significantly influenced by steel composition, particularly the phase transformations occurring during rapid quenching of AISI 4340 alloy steel.

✓ Vegetable Oils

Sunflower and soybean oils exhibited lower maximum cooling rates compared to mineral oil, reducing thermal shock while achieving comparable hardening depth in 4340 steel.

✓ Residual Stress

Vegetable oils produced smaller magnitude residual stresses, which is beneficial for fatigue life in high-strength components such as gears, springs and shafts.
