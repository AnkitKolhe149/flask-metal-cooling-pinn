🧮 How the Predictions are Generated

⚛️ PINN

The trained PINN network takes [τ, material, oil, T_bath, T₀] as input and predicts θ. The θ is back-converted to physical temperature: T = T_bath + θ·(T₀ − T_bath). The PINN enforces the ODE constraint during training so predictions satisfy Newton's cooling even between training points.

🤖 DNN

Identical input/output structure to PINN but no physics term in loss. The DNN may deviate from the exponential cooling profile, especially near τ = 0 (film boiling phase) or for unseen oil/temperature combinations outside the training distribution.

📐 Analytical

Using the PINN's learned β: T(τ) = T_bath + (T₀ − T_bath)·e^(−β·τ). This is the exact solution to Newton's cooling law and serves as a physics baseline. When the PINN and analytical curves align closely, it confirms the physics constraint is being respected.
