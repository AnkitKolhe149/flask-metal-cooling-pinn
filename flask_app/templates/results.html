🧾 Result Interpretation & Key Findings

✅ PINN Advantages

  • Physics-consistent predictions
  • Learns interpretable β coefficient
  • Better generalisation to unseen conditions
  • Enforces θ(τ=0) = 1 boundary condition
  • Tighter error distribution near zero
  • No thermodynamically absurd predictions

⚠️ DNN Limitations

  • May violate energy conservation
  • No physical interpretability
  • Requires more data for same accuracy
  • Can predict rising temperatures during quench
  • No learned physical parameter
  • Extrapolation to new conditions is risky

📐 Dataset-Specific Observations

  • AISI 4340 harder to predict (phase transforms)
  • Higher bath temps → smaller temperature gradients
  • Sunflower & soy behave similarly to each other
  • Initial ~10 s most nonlinear (film boiling)
  • Prediction accuracy improves at τ > 0.3
  • 80°C bath curves easiest to predict
